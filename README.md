
1. Regulatory & Legal Requirements

Licensing & Registration: Research required licenses (SEC, FINRA, FCA, etc.) depending on your country.

KYC & AML Compliance: Know Your Customer and Anti-Money Laundering procedures are mandatory.

Data Privacy Laws: GDPR, CCPA, or relevant local laws for handling user data.

Partnerships with Financial Institutions: Agreements with banks, brokers, or custodians.



---

2. Business Model Planning

Investment Types Offered: Stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, crypto, real estate, etc.

Revenue Model: Commission-based, subscription, percentage of assets under management, etc.

Target Audience: Retail investors, high-net-worth individuals, beginners, etc.



---

3. Financial and Market Data

Real-Time Market Feeds: Stock/crypto prices, indices, forex data.

Historical Data: Price charts, earnings reports, economic indicators.

Third-Party APIs: Alpha Vantage, IEX Cloud, Yahoo Finance, Bloomberg, etc.



---

4. Technical Infrastructure

Frontend App: Mobile (iOS/Android) and/or web platform.

Backend Server: Secure server for user data, transactions, and app logic.

Trading Engine or Broker API: For executing trades (e.g., Alpaca, Interactive Brokers).

Database: For user profiles, portfolio data, transactions, and logs.

Security Protocols: Encryption, 2FA, secure storage for PII and financial data.



---

5. User Features & Tools

User Dashboard: Portfolio overview, profit/loss tracking.

Investment Options: Buy/sell interface, asset search, filters.

Risk Assessment Tools: Questionnaires, automated profiling.

News & Analysis Feed: Financial news, stock analysis, learning resources.

Customer Support: In-app chat, ticketing system.



---

6. Design and User Experience

UI/UX Design: Clean, intuitive, and responsive interface.

Onboarding Flow: Easy account setup, verification, and deposit process.

Notifications System: Alerts for prices, portfolio changes, news, etc.



---

7. Testing and Audit

Security Audits: Penetration testing, third-party review.

Beta Testing: Controlled user testing for feedback and bug fixes.

Performance Testing: Load testing, latency, and uptime analysis.



---

8. Marketing and Launch Strategy

Branding: Logo, name, mission, values.

Marketing Plan: SEO, ads, influencers, partnerships.

Launch Readiness: Documentation, customer support, FAQs, social presence.
